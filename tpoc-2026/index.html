One More Hour

Somewhere in a windowless office, a system known only as the Calendar has locked away the extra hour that fall gives back, so the clocks roll on exactly as scheduled. No delays. No grace period. Not one spare minute.

Your crew has one shot: break three seals before the countdown runs out, and take the hour back.

Three rooms. One crew. Playing together, call out answers as a group. Playing alone, you are the whole crew.

Before you go live

    
Four tips

    
    
      
  1. Prompt for accessibility. Ask for WCAG 2.1 AA from the start. Retrofitting accessibility is much harder than building it in.
    2. 
      
  2. Test and revise. The first output is a draft. Play it as a student, check what reaches the gradebook, then have a real student try it. Plan at least one revision round.
    3. 
      
  3. Control your SCORM ending. Tell the AI to hold on the final screen instead of rolling to a completion slide. Students stay in the activity until they are ready to exit.
    4. 
      
  4. Balance multiple choice answers. On multiple choice questions, tell the AI to make the answer choices similar in length so the longest answer is not always the correct one, and to shuffle the correct answer across all of the letter choices (A, B, C and D).
